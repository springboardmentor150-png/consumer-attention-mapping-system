1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VISION
====================================================================================================
The Consumer Attention Mapping System (CAMS) is an enterprise-grade spatial intelligence and visual
analytics platform that converts raw, passive retail CCTV video streams into actionable, quantitative
merchandising and store layout intelligence.

By combining State-of-the-Art Computer Vision (YOLO Object Detection + DeepSORT Multi-Object Tracking),
an asynchronous Python FastAPI backend, a single-source-of-truth PostgreSQL relational database, and an
interactive Next.js 14 executive dashboard, CAMS calculates:
- Exact customer dwell time per shelf zone.
- Visual attention hotspots and ignored "dead zones".
- Multi-metric attractiveness scores (0 to 100 benchmark index).
- Explainable merchandising recommendations (pricing barriers, conversion leakage, visual appeal).

====================================================================================================
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT & RETAIL MARKET GAP
====================================================================================================
Physical brick-and-mortar retail accounts for over 80% of global shopping transactions, yet store
planners suffer from critical blind spots compared to e-commerce:

- E-COMMERCE: Tracks clicks, hover time, scroll depth, session journeys, and drop-off funnels.
- TRADITIONAL PHYSICAL RETAIL: Only knows what was purchased at the cash register.
- THE MISSING METRIC: Retailers have zero visibility into what customers looked at, touched, and
  walked away from without purchasing.
- CAMS SOLUTION: Leverages existing overhead CCTV camera networks to unlock digital-grade in-store
  behavioral analytics without requiring expensive RFID tags, smart carts, or wearable sensors.

4. ALL MATHEMATICAL MODELS & CALCULATION FORMULAS
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 YOLO BOUNDING BOX & CENTROID COORDINATE FORMULA
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When YOLO detects a shopper in video frame `f`, it outputs a bounding box:
    BBox = [x_min, y_min, x_max, y_max]

The spatial centroid (center of mass representing shopper standing position) is calculated as:
    x_centroid = (x_min + x_max) / 2
    y_centroid = (y_min + y_max) / 2

Bottom-Center Ground Footprint (for accurate floorplan grounding):
    x_foot = (x_min + x_max) / 2
    y_foot = y_max

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 PIXEL-TO-FLOORPLAN NORMALIZATION & SPATIAL MAPPING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To make spatial tracking resolution-independent (whether the camera is 720p, 1080p, or 4K):
    x_norm = x_pixel / W_video        (where x_norm in [0.0, 1.0])
    y_norm = y_pixel / H_video        (where y_norm in [0.0, 1.0])

Translation to 2D Store Floorplan Canvas (Canvas width W_canvas, height H_canvas):
    X_canvas = x_norm * W_canvas
    Y_canvas = y_norm * H_canvas

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 SPATIAL POINT-IN-SHELF BOUNDING BOX INTERSECTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A shelf zone `S` is defined by normalized spatial boundaries: [Sx1, Sy1, Sx2, Sy2].
A shopper detection point `P(x_norm, y_norm)` belongs to Shelf `S` if and only if:
    In_Zone(P, S) = { 1,  if (Sx1 <= x_norm <= Sx2) AND (Sy1 <= y_norm <= Sy2)
                    { 0,  otherwise

Total Dwell Duration (in seconds) for Track `T` at Shelf `S`:
    Dwell(T, S) = (Sum_{frame in T} In_Zone(P_frame, S)) / FPS_video

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.4 5x8 GRID HEATMAP DWELL TIME & DENSITY EQUATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The camera viewing field is discretized into a matrix of M = 5 Rows (Vertical Shelf Levels) by
N = 8 Columns (Aisle Horizontal Sections), creating 40 individual cells:
    Cell(r, c) where r in [0, 4] and c in [0, 7]

Cell Spatial Boundaries:
    r_idx = floor(y_norm * 5)
    c_idx = floor(x_norm * 8)

Cumulative Cell Dwell Time (seconds):
    Dwell_Cell(r, c) = (Total frames where detections fall in Cell(r, c)) * (1.0 / FPS)

Normalized Cell Intensity:
    Intensity_Cell(r, c) = Dwell_Cell(r, c) / max(Dwell_Cell(all cells))  in [0.0, 1.0]

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.5 SCIENTIFIC YlOrRd COLOR PALETTE PIECEWISE INTERPOLATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CAMS applies the 7-stop YlOrRd (Yellow-Orange-Red) colormap equation across normalized intensity `v`:
    - v = 0.0 -> #ffffb2 (Light Yellow, minimal traffic)
    - v = 0.2 -> #fed976 (Soft Yellow-Orange)
    - v = 0.4 -> #feb24c (Orange-Amber)
    - v = 0.6 -> #fd8d3c (Warm Orange)
    - v = 0.8 -> #f03b20 (Bright Orange-Red)
    - v = 1.0 -> #bd0026 (Deep Ruby Red, peak attention hotspot)

Linear Channel Interpolation between bounding stops [v_0, v_1] with colors [C_0, C_1]:
    t = (v - v_0) / (v_1 - v_0)
    RGB(v) = round( (1 - t) * RGB(C_0) + t * RGB(C_1) )

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.6 THE 5-METRIC SHELF ATTRACTIVENESS SCORING FORMULATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The standardized Shelf Attractiveness Score evaluates every physical retail shelf on a 0.0 to 100.0
objective scale:

    Score = [ (0.35 * Attention_Norm) + 
              (0.25 * Interaction_Norm) + 
              (0.20 * Pickup_Rate) + 
              (0.15 * Conversion_Rate) + 
              (0.05 * Repeat_Rate) ] * 100

Where:
1. Attention Time Factor (35% Weight):
       Attention_Norm = Dwell_Shelf / max_{all shelves}(Dwell_Shelf)
       Points Contributed = 0.35 * Attention_Norm * 100   (Max: 35.0 pts)

2. Interaction Frequency Factor (25% Weight):
       Interaction_Norm = Visits_Shelf / max_{all shelves}(Visits_Shelf)
       Points Contributed = 0.25 * Interaction_Norm * 100 (Max: 25.0 pts)

3. Physical Pickup Rate Factor (20% Weight):
       Pickup_Rate = (Visits with Dwell >= 5.0 seconds) / (Total Unique Visitors to Shelf)
       Points Contributed = 0.20 * Pickup_Rate * 100      (Max: 20.0 pts)

4. Purchase Conversion Rate Factor (15% Weight):
       Conversion_Rate = (Visits with Dwell >= 10.0 seconds) / (Total Unique Visitors to Shelf)
       Points Contributed = 0.15 * Conversion_Rate * 100  (Max: 15.0 pts)

5. Repeat Engagement Rate Factor (5% Weight):
       Repeat_Rate = (Visits with Dwell >= 15.0 seconds) / (Total Unique Visitors to Shelf)
       Points Contributed = 0.05 * Repeat_Rate * 100      (Max: 5.0 pts)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.7 BEHAVIORAL THRESHOLD FORMULATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Pass-by Traffic: Dwell < 3.0 seconds (Shopper walked past without sustained gaze).
- Meaningful Interaction: Dwell >= 3.0 seconds (Shopper paused and focused attention).
- Physical Inspection / Pickup: Dwell >= 5.0 seconds (Physical product handling or reading specs).
- High-Intent Conversion: Dwell >= 10.0 seconds (Cart placement or deep comparison).
- Return / Loyalty Re-Engagement: Dwell >= 15.0 seconds (Extended multi-product browsing).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.8 STORE NETWORK AGGREGATION & CROSS-STORE KPI INDEXING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Overall Store Average Attractiveness Score (for Store K with N registered shelves):
    Store_Score_K = (1 / N) * Sum_{i=1}^{N} Score(Shelf_i)

Store Average Dwell Duration:
    Avg_Dwell_Store = (Total Dwell of all Person Tracks in Store) / (Total Unique Person Tracks)

6. RULE-BASED MERCHANDISING OPTIMIZATION ENGINE
====================================================================================================
CAMS diagnoses behavioral patterns into natural-language retail directives:

- HIGH DWELL + LOW PICKUP:
  "Price/Packaging Barrier: Customers spend time viewing products but rarely pick them up.
   Action: Re-evaluate shelf price tags, improve font clarity, or introduce promotional banners."

- HIGH PICKUP + LOW CONVERSION:
  "Conversion Leakage: Customers pick up items but fail to finalize purchase.
   Action: Address product size/quality concerns or add prominent value-proposition callouts."

- LOW ATTENTION + HIGH TRAFFIC AISLE:
  "Visual Appeal Deficit: High footfall aisle with low shelf dwell.
   Action: Enhance shelf lighting, use contrast shelf talkers, or reposition slow SKUs to endcaps."

- HIGH OVERALL ATTRACTIVENESS (> 60 Score):
  "Prime Performer: High customer engagement across all 5 metrics.
   Action: Utilize this shelf for high-margin new product launches and brand partnerships."
